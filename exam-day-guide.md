# SAA-C03 Exam-Day Guide

This page covers the logistics of the AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03) exam: how to book it, how the two delivery options differ, what to have ready, how the exam is built and scored, and what happens after you finish. Every fact below links to the official AWS page it came from. AWS updates its policies from time to time, so confirm anything time-sensitive on the linked page before your appointment.

> Also see the [SAA-C03 practice questions](https://www.savemycert.com/practice/aws-solutions-architect-associate/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=saa-c03-study-guide) and [timed mock exams](https://www.savemycert.com/mocks/aws-solutions-architect-associate/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=saa-c03-study-guide).

## Booking the exam

To register, sign in to aws.training, open Certification in the top navigation, choose AWS Certification Account, then Schedule New Exam. Find SAA-C03 and choose Schedule with Pearson VUE to pick a location, date, and time and pay.

The exam costs 150 USD. It is offered in English, French (France), Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish (Latin America), Spanish (Spain), Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. AWS notes that the Italian version retires after December 31, 2026.

If your plans change, you can reschedule up to 24 hours before your appointment, and each appointment can be rescheduled only twice. Cancellations must also happen at least 24 hours ahead. Canceling with less than 24 hours' notice, or not showing up, forfeits your exam fee.

Source: [AWS Certification FAQs](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/faqs/), [AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-solutions-architect-associate/), [Before Testing](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/before-testing/)

## Online proctored vs test center

You can take SAA-C03 at a Pearson VUE testing center or online with a proctor. The rules differ in ways that matter:

- **Test center:** arrive 15 to 30 minutes before your appointment. You give your name, the exam title, and your ID, sign a candidate agreement, and may be photographed. Unscheduled breaks are allowed, but the exam timer keeps running and you cannot leave the building.
- **Online proctored:** you can launch the exam up to 30 minutes before your scheduled time and check in yourself, including ID and environment checks, then connect with a proctor to begin. If you are more than 15 minutes late, or your computer fails the system test, you cannot launch the exam and your fee is forfeited. You may not stand up or leave the camera's view for any reason during the exam, including for a restroom break.

Source: [During testing (test day)](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/during-testing/), [Before Testing](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/before-testing/)

## What to have ready

**ID.** The name on your ID must match your AWS Certification Account. For an online proctored exam you need one primary ID. At a test center you need two primary IDs, or one primary and one secondary ID. If you do not have a qualifying government-issued ID from the country where you test (EU ID holders excepted), AWS requires your passport as the primary ID, plus a secondary ID. The rules are detailed, so check the current policy before exam day.

**System and room check (online).** Run Pearson VUE's system test on the computer you will use before your appointment. AWS's pages do not list the full workspace rules. Check Pearson VUE's current online testing requirements, linked from the AWS policy page, well before exam day.

Source: [During testing (test day)](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/during-testing/), [Before Testing](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/before-testing/)

## How the exam is structured

You get 130 minutes for 65 questions. There are two question types:

- **Multiple choice:** one correct response and three incorrect responses (distractors).
- **Multiple response:** two or more correct responses out of five or more options.

Only 50 of the 65 questions count toward your score. The other 15 are unscored questions that AWS is evaluating for future use, and they are not marked on the exam, so treat every question as if it counts. Unanswered questions are scored as incorrect, and there is no penalty for guessing.

The exam uses abbreviated AWS service names, with a full reference list available through the Help button during the exam.

The scored content is split across four domains: Design Secure Architectures (30%), Design Resilient Architectures (26%), Design High-Performing Architectures (24%), and Design Cost-Optimized Architectures (20%).

Source: [AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03) exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/solutions-architect-associate-03/solutions-architect-associate-03.html), [AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-solutions-architect-associate/)

## How scoring works

The exam is pass or fail. Your result is reported as a scaled score from 100 to 1,000, and the minimum passing score is 720. Scaled scoring equates results across exam forms of slightly different difficulty, so 720 is a point on the scale, not "72% of questions correct."

The exam uses a compensatory scoring model. You do not need to pass each section, only the exam as a whole. Your score report may include a per-section table. Treat it as a general signal of strengths and weaknesses, not a precise measurement.

Source: [AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03) exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/solutions-architect-associate-03/solutions-architect-associate-03.html)

## Results and your digital badge

Most AWS Certification exams do not show a pass or fail result at the end of the test. Your results appear in your AWS Certification Account, under Exam History, within five business days, and you get an email when they are ready. You can download a PDF of your score report there.

If you pass, AWS issues a digital badge through Credly. The Credly email can arrive before your results show up in your account. You can share the badge on social media, embed it on a website, and verify it through Credly. The certification is valid for three years. Earning the Solutions Architect – Professional certification also recertifies it.

Source: [AWS Certification FAQs](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/faqs/), [After Testing](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/after-testing/), [AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-solutions-architect-associate/)

## If you don't pass

You must wait 14 calendar days before you can retake the exam. There is no limit on attempts, but you pay the full registration fee each time. Once you pass, you cannot retake the same exam for two years.

Source: [After Testing](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/after-testing/)

## On exam day: practical tips

- **Pace yourself.** 65 questions in 130 minutes is about 2 minutes each. Keep moving and leave time at the end for review.
- **Flag and move on.** If a question drags, pick your best answer, flag it, and come back. Never leave one blank, since a guess costs nothing.
- **Eliminate first.** Distractors are designed to look plausible. Cross out options that fail a stated requirement.
- **Read for the constraint.** Words like "most cost-effective," "least operational overhead," "highly available," or "most secure" usually decide between two workable answers.
